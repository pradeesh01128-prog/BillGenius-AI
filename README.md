# 🧾 BillGenius AI – Smart Billing & Retail Management System

**BillGenius AI** is an AI-powered billing and retail management application designed for shops, supermarkets, and small businesses. It simplifies billing, product management, inventory tracking, sales analysis, and business operations using Artificial Intelligence.

The application includes smart product scanning, voice-based billing, automated bill calculations, stock monitoring, and sales analytics to improve accuracy and save time.

## 🚀 Features

- 🧾 **Smart Billing System** – Generate bills with automatic price and total calculations.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Assistance** – Use AI features to support billing and retail operations.
- 📷 **AI Vision Scanner** – Scan products using a camera-based interface.
- 🔊 **Voice Billing** – Add products through voice commands.
- 🏷️ **Barcode Scanner** – Support barcode-based product identification.
- 📦 **Inventory Management** – Manage product stock and monitor inventory levels.
- ⚠️ **Low Stock Alerts** – Identify products that need restocking.
- 📊 **Sales Analytics Dashboard** – View sales and revenue information.
- 👥 **Staff Management** – Manage staff-related information and performance.
- 🏪 **Branch Management** – Monitor inventory across branches.
- 💰 **Profit Calculator** – Calculate business profit using sales and cost information.
- 📄 **Receipt Generation** – Generate billing receipts for customers.
- 📈 **Reports Export** – Export business reports for analysis.
- 🇮🇳 **Indian Currency Support** – Designed for billing in Indian Rupees (₹).
